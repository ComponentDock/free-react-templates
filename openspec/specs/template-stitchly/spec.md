# Stitchly — Tailor & Sewing Services Template

Recreation of ColorLib "Tailor" (https://colorlib.com/wp/template/tailor/).

## Design Tokens

- Brand color: #BE9278 (warm copper/tan)
- Secondary: #6785FF (blue accent)
- Dark: #072366 (navy headings/footer)
- Near-black: #16161a
- Light backgrounds: #F5F9FB, #f7f7fd, #f1f4fa, #f9fafc
- Fonts: Josefin Sans (headings), Montserrat (body)
- Buttons: rounded-none (square), #BE9278 bg, white text, uppercase

## Sections (in order)

1. **Navbar** — sticky header, logo + nav links (Home, About, Services, Blog, Contact) + "Visit Us" CTA button
2. **Hero** — full-width hero with image background, rotating icon, heading "We make clothes that suit you", subtext, "Our Services" CTA button
3. **Offers** — 3-column cards with numbered icons: Tailor Sewing, Measurement, Ready-made
4. **About** — split layout: image left, "About our tailor house" text + CTA right
5. **Services** — 4-column icon grid: "Why use our service?" heading, 4 service cards
6. **Video** — dark background section with play button (link to YouTube)
7. **Testimonial** — quote card with founder image and name
8. **Instagram** — 2 gallery images with Instagram icon overlay
9. **CTA Banner** — "Book Your Appointment" section with schedule button
10. **Footer** — 4-column: logo+social, Quick Links, Services, Opening Hours+Contact; Component Dock link

## Scenarios

- Navbar renders all nav links and Visit Us button
- Hero section displays heading and CTA
- Offers section shows 3 service cards
- About section displays split layout with CTA
- Services section shows 4 service categories
- Testimonial section displays quote and author
- Footer links to Component Dock
- Mobile menu toggles open/close

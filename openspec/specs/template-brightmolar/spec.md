# Template: BrightMolar (Healthcare / Dental Clinic)

## Purpose

Recreation of ColorLib `toothcare` (`https://preview.colorlib.com/theme/toothcare/`).

- **New Name**: BrightMolar (`brightmolar`)
- **Category**: Healthcare / Dental Clinic / Medical
- **Stack**: Vite, React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, TypeScript, Lucide Icons.

## Design Tokens

- **Primary Brand Color**: Cyan / Teal (`#00bcd4`, `#65c4cf`, Tailwind `cyan-500` / `teal-400`)
- **Secondary / Dark Color**: Dark Charcoal (`#212529`, `#111111`)
- **Backgrounds**: Light gray (`#f8f9fa`), white (`#ffffff`), dark teal/slate footer.
- **Font Family**: "Roboto", sans-serif.
- **Border Radius**: 4px / `0.25rem` / rounded-md.

## Sections

1. **Navbar**: Logo, Navigation Links (Home, Dentist, About, Services, Pricing, Blog, Contact), CTA "MAKE AN APPOINTMENT" button.
2. **Hero / Slider**: Headline ("A Brighter Dental Experienced"), subtext, appointment CTA buttons, carousel indicators.
3. **Appointment Form & Features Highlights**: Quick booking form (Name, Email, Phone, Date, Time, Doctor select, Message, Submit) combined with service highlight cards.
4. **Services Grid**: Tooth Protection, Dental Implants, Dental Care, Teeth Whitening, Dental Calculus, Tooth Removal, Removal of Tartar, Tooth Inspection.
5. **About / Intro Banner**: "Toothcare is a Dental Clinic and Innovative Approach to Treatment" with background image and CTA.
6. **Stats Counter**: 3000+ Happy Patients, 2200+ Successful Surgeries, 24/7 Support.
7. **Team / Dentists**: Dr. Lloyd Wilson (Head Dentist), Dr. Rachel Parker, Dr. Ian Smith, Dr. Alicia Henderson with social links and roles.
8. **CTA Banner**: "Do not wait make an appointment today!" with button.
9. **Testimonials / Happy Customers**: Carousel of patient reviews.
10. **Treatment Results Gallery**: Visual showcase of dental work results.
11. **Blog / Recent Posts**: Dental health tips ("How to keep your teeth always in the best condition").
12. **Pricing & Packages**: Service tier cards with "GET STARTED" buttons.
13. **Footer**: Clinic info, Quick links, Recent posts, Contact info, and mandatory Component Dock attribution ("Made with Component Dock" linking `https://www.componentdock.com/`).

## Gherkin Requirements & Scenarios

### Feature: BrightMolar Landing Page

- Scenario: User loads the landing page and views the header navigation and hero section.
- Scenario: User fills out and submits the appointment booking form with valid data.
- Scenario: User browses the dentist team members and their social profiles.
- Scenario: User explores the dental services grid and pricing packages.
- Scenario: User scrolls to the footer and verifies the Component Dock attribution link.

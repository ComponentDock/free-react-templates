# App ↔ live palette — does each shipped app paint its reference's colours?

```sh
python3 scripts/verify-app-live-tokens.py \
  --cache /tmp/live-tokens-cache --jobs 8 \
  --md docs/app-live-tokens.md --json /tmp/app-live.json
python3 scripts/verify-app-live-tokens-controls.py   # controls
```

Generated by `scripts/verify-app-live-tokens.py` (read-only; it never
edits `apps/`). It is the third edge of the token triangle: the two
shipped axes compare a **spec** with its live sheet
(`verify-live-tokens`) and with its app (`verify-app-fidelity`).
Neither can see a hole both agree on, and neither asks whether the
app's brand hue exists in the reference at all.

_Provenance: measured 2026-10-24 over 1,703 apps (cache-only; the live fetches were made the same day)_

Corpus: **1703** shipped apps (`apps/<name>/src`).

## Method

* **Live side** — `catalog_resolve` → the demo's THEME-owned
  stylesheets only (vendor/library bundles excluded; the vendor test
  is scoped below the demo directory, so `/theme/bootstrap/<slug>/`
  counts as a demo family, and a `css/gijgo.css`-style bundle is
  excluded by name). Every declaration is weighted — background 3,
  text colour 2, border 1 — and **CSS variables are resolved**, so a
  theme that defines `--accent` and paints `var(--accent)` is read
  (the first draft, which counted literals, called 103 of 300 apps
  `NO-DESIGN-COLOUR`).
* **App side** — every hex (and `rgb()` form) in
  `apps/<name>/src/**`; the app's brand is its `--color-brand`
  anchor, else the most-painted member of its `@theme` palette, else
  its most-painted chromatic colour.
* **Same hue family** — HSV hue within 20°: `#d6a04c` and
  `#b98a3c` are one family (a re-picked shade), a coral and a
  corporate blue are not. A shade is a note (`SHADE-DRIFT`).
* **LIVE-UNPAINTED** — the design's LEADING colour (most-painted,
  painted at section scale) has no app colour within
  Δ24 and no member of its hue family in the app.
* **REHUE / EXTRA-HUE** — the app's brand is a hue the live sheets
  never paint: `REHUE` (note) when it also paints none of the
  design's families — a different design — and `EXTRA-HUE` (note)
  when the design's palette is painted and a hue was added. Neither
  is a bucket: a missing lead is `LIVE-UNPAINTED`.
* **Shared scaffold** — a colour that is a palette candidate in ≥
  20 unrelated previews is the host's scaffold, not any
  design's signature (`#38a4ff` in 67 of 594 sampled, the
  `contact-form-*` family's `#ccda46` in 6). Excluded from being a
  leading colour; `--no-scaffold` is the A/B control.
* **Library state ramp** — a shade of a framework default (Δ ≤ 24
  from a Bootstrap/Bulma/Material colour) that is painted ONLY by a
  framework component in a state pseudo-class is the library's own
  ramp, not a design surface: Bootstrap 5's `.btn-primary:hover`
  `#0b5ed7` is Δ13 from its `#0d6efd`. Reading it as design
  manufactured three LIVE-ACCENT-MISSING notes against apps that
  paint their design correctly; `--no-fw-shade` is the A/B control.
* **Dismissed as not-a-palette** — a colour painted only under
  `:hover`/`:focus`, only by `::selection`, only by a library widget's
  chrome (`.nice-select`, `[role="handle"]`), only by a share row's
  positional `li:nth-child(k)` icon, or only by a kit block the page
  never instantiates (`.genric-btn.primary`); a text-selection colour
  and an unrendered kit button are not design decisions.
* A rebuilt demo (`?v=3.x` kit, a hashed `Base.DSJ9xNgp.css` bundle, a
  compiled Tailwind sheet) is drift, not debt: `RESKIN` /
  `RESKIN-KEPT`, never a flag.
* A colour-variant BUTTON kit (`bootstrap-buttons-*`: one hue per
  flavour, painted only through control selectors) has no single
  brand: `NO-DESIGN-COLOUR` with a `KIT-SHEET` note.

## Reading a hit

* **The residual is two shapes, both visible in the table's `prep
  category` column.** A page design the app re-skinned
  (`alignwell`: the reference paints `#01d28e` on `.btn-primary`,
  `.bg-primary` and the nav; the app ships Bootstrap blue only) is app
  debt: an implementer restyle, never an edit from this clone.
* A widget/kit subject (`(Accordion UI Component)`, `(Contact Form)`,
  `(UI Component Showcase & Buttons Gallery)`) is `KIT-DEMO`: its sheet
  paints one control accent, the app is a page, and "the page does not
  paint the widget's accent" is not a fidelity statement.
* The reference is not the design any more (`RESKIN*`: a `?v=3.x` kit,
  a hashed `Base.xxxxxxxx.css` bundle) — drift to adjudicate in the
  spec, never app debt.
* The colour is not the design's at all — a framework default, a
  social row's icon, a `::selection`, an unrendered kit block: those
  are dismissed before the comparison and never reach this table.
* **Why this read is additive (measured 2026-10-24).** 13 of the 30
  flagged apps are already `UNSHIPPED` rows of
  `docs/app-fidelity-delta.md`; the other 17 are invisible to that
  axis — 7 are demoted to `APPROX-VARIANT` because the spec's usage
  cell mentions a `:hover` rule (a Δ>100 miss becomes a note), 8 are
  `COVERED` because the app paints the colours the spec claimed and
  the claim set never held the design's lead, and 2 carry no
  brand-role claim at all. 41 colour rows underlie the 30 apps: 31
  the prep recorded (app debt) and 10 it never recorded (prep debt).
* `--no-scaffold`, `--scaffold-min N` and `--no-fw-shade` are the
  A/B controls for the corpus-level scaffold and library-state guards;
  `--show BUCKET` prints any bucket.

# App-side fidelity delta — does each app paint the measured brand?

```sh
python3 scripts/verify-app-fidelity.py --md docs/app-fidelity-delta.md
python3 scripts/verify-app-fidelity-controls.py   # 19/19
```

Generated by `scripts/verify-app-fidelity.py` (offline, read-only; it
never edits `apps/`). Every other token axis compares a spec against
its live reference sheet. This one compares the shipped app's own
palette against the brand-role colours its prep measured.

_Provenance: after the brand-claim backfill: 267 preps gained a machine-readable Brand row (docs/brand-claims-coverage.md), 2026-10-23_

Corpus: **3230** specs, **1703** of them
with a shipped app (`apps/<name>/src`), **2110** brand-role
colour claims evaluated.

## Method

* **Claim side** — the brand-role rows/bullets of
  `openspec/specs/template-<name>/spec.md` whose role label contains
  `brand|primary|accent|cta|secondary` (variant labels such as `hover`,
  `scale`, `tint` excluded; evidence fences, sibling sentences,
  re-valuation arrows and rebuilt-sheet drift notes skipped).
* **App side** — every hex in `apps/<name>/src/**` (`.css`, `.tsx`,
  `.ts`, `.html`), matched in hex or `rgb()/rgba()` notation.
* **UNSHIPPED** — a measured brand colour with no counterpart in the
  app, nearest app chromatic more than **24** RGB units away (max
  channel). Closer neighbours are `APPROX` notes: the app paints a
  drift, not a different colour.
* **UNDOCUMENTED-ANCHOR** — `--color-brand` / `--color-accent` /
  `--color-secondary` holds a hex no spec token states and no spec hex
  is within 24 units of: the app invented a brand colour.
* An app whose `src` holds **no chromatic hex** (Tailwind named-palette
  utilities only) is `NO-TOKEN`: not comparable from source, not a
  defect. A spec with no brand-role colour is `NO-CLAIM`. Alias preps
  (`prep-status: shipped-as=`) are out of scope.

## Reading the numbers

Severity is the max-channel distance between the measured colour and
the closest the app paints:

| Δ | claims | what it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25–49 | 31 | a re-picked shade of the same hue |
| 50–89 | 33 | a neighbouring colour family |
| 90+ | 61 | a different colour: the design's accent was replaced |

A colour that recurs across apps is one shortcut, not N mistakes: the
most repeated here is `#007bff`,
which several apps replaced with a different blue instead of the measured
one.

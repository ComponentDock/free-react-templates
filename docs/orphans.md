# Prep folders that own no app (freeze index)

Generated by `index-orphans.py` in the prep-stream skill; do not hand-edit.
Regenerate with:

    python3 ~/.hermes/skills/automation/componentdock-prep-stream/scripts/index-orphans.py

## What this is

`openspec/specs/template-<name>/` exists for 3230 folders but only 1703 of them
own an `apps/<name>`. The other **1527** are prep history: a spec (and usually a
`docs/templates/<name>/` brief) written for a design that shipped under a different
invented name, or for a design that has no app at all. Nothing here is wasted work —
it is research that a second prep would otherwise have to redo — but nothing here is
the fidelity reference for a shipped app either: that is always the app's own
`openspec/specs/template-<app>/spec.md`.

**Freeze decision (2026-10-23k):** keep them, enumerate them, delete nothing. The
1,470 duplicates could be folded (git history keeps every one, and only 8 are
referenced by another spec), but a ~3,000-file deletion is the owner's call, not an
unattended run's. `scripts/verify-prep-directive.py` is the gate that keeps a
duplicate from handing out build work for a folder that does not exist.

## Reading a line

    template-<folder>   <source slug>   -> apps/<app>  (row N, <proof>)

`proof` is `banner` (the prep already carries a SHIPPED-UNDER banner), `row`
(the row's app cites the same slug — proven, no banner read), or `unproven`
(the row links an app, but ownership is the slug MENTION only, or that app cites a
different slug — a human read is still owed).
